Reality check 💡

Where is a full ride easiest?

  • 1. CUNY / SUNY (in-state) — Excelsior + TAP + Pell = $0 for most NYC families.
  • 2. Wealthy private + Ivies — meet 100% of need; under ~$100K often free.
  • 3. Top liberal arts — no-loan aid + QuestBridge full rides.
  • 4. HBCUs — affordable but smaller endowments; expect to stack outside scholarships.

Lesson: apply to a mix — the cheapest path is often a name you didn't expect.

College Strategy

What admissions actually weigh

  • GPA + rigor — grades in challenging classes you can handle.
  • Extracurriculars — depth and impact beat a long list.
  • Essays — your voice, your story, your "why."
  • Recommendations — teachers who know you well.
  • Fit — they want students who'll thrive there.
Build a balanced list

Reach · Target · Likely

Reach

Dream schools. Your stats are below or at the low end. Apply to a few.

Target

Your numbers fit the typical admit. The heart of your list.

Likely

Strong odds + you'd happily attend. Always include some.

Aim for ~8–12 schools spread across all three.

Paying for it

FAFSA + CSS Profile

FAFSA

  • Free federal aid form
  • Grants, loans, work-study
  • Required by every school

CSS Profile

  • Used by many private schools
  • Deeper financial picture
  • More institutional aid

Cost is a strategy question — never rule a school out before you see your aid.

Part 2 · Personal Narrative

The essay is not a résumé

Your grades and activities are already on the application. The essay's job is to make an admissions officer feel who you are. That comes from a story.

  • 650 words, one human, one voice.
  • They read thousands — yours stands out by being specific.
  • Show growth, not perfection.
From the video

The 5 keys to a great narrative

  • Pick a defining moment — small + everyday is fine.
  • Show growth — what changed in how you think.
  • Be specific — details, dialogue, senses. Show, don't tell.
  • Sound like you — not what you think they want.
  • Connect to the future — who you'll be on campus.
Remember

"The best topic isn't the most dramatic — it's the most you." Show a moment. Reflect. Reveal who you are.
